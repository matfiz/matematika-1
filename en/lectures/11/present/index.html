Lecture 11

Taylor approximation and small oscillations

A Taylor polynomial matches several derivatives at one point. The remainder formula turns that matching into an error estimate. We prove the formula, compare the meanings of bounded and negligible remainders, and examine a function with derivatives of every order whose Taylor coefficients all vanish. A quadratic potential approximation then gives a linearised mechanical equation with a stated Newton-law premise.
